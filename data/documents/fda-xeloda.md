# XELODA drug label

5.8 Palmar-Plantar Erythrodysesthesia Syndrome Palmar-plantar erythrodysesthesia syndrome (PPES) can occur with XELODA. In patients with metastatic breast or colorectal cancer who received XELODA as a single agent, the median time to onset of grades 1 to 3 PPES was 2.6 months (range: 11 days to 1 year). Withhold XELODA and then resume at same or reduced dose or permanently discontinue based on severity and occurrence [see Dosage and Administration (2.5) ] .
